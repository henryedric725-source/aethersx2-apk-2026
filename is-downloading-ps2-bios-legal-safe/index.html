FAQ

Is ps2 bios legal to download?

Downloading Sony firmware from random sites is copyright infringement in most countries. Dumping from your own console is the supported approach.

Is downloading ps2 bios safe?

Unverified downloads may include truncated or malicious files. Verify 4,194,304-byte size and SHA-256.
